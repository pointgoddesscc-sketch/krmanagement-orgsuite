Business management services for modern workplaces.

KR Management (legal display name Krmanagement, dashboard name MANAGEMENT TEAM) sells professional management and operations services. OrgSuite is the software and coordination layer we use to run workplace systems, documentation, and paid retainers.

We are a management-services business. We are not a bank, money transmitter, or depository institution. Customer card payments are processed by Stripe.

What we sell

These are the goods and services offered under this Stripe account (MCC 7392 — management services):

  • Workplace operations

    Retainer-based operations support: scheduling coordination, vendor follow-up, documentation hygiene, and project tracking for small teams.

  • OrgSuite workspace setup

    Paid setup of OrgSuite workplace tooling: connected inboxes, task boards, public pages, and internal operating checklists.

  • Consulting and process design

    Written operating procedures, connector checklists, and implementation plans for multi-tool workplaces.

  • Invoice collection support

    Professional invoicing and collection coordination for agreed client work. Funds move through Stripe Checkout or Payment Links when a client pays.

How payment works

  1. We agree scope in writing (email or signed note).
  2. We send a Stripe Payment Link or hosted invoice for the quoted amount.
  3. The client pays by card or other Stripe-supported method.
  4. Work starts after payment confirmation, unless a written milestone plan says otherwise.

Statement descriptor on cards: KR MANAGEMENT.

Refund and cancellation policy

  • Unused prepaid retainers canceled in writing within 14 days of payment are refunded in full to the original payment method.
  • After work has started, refunds cover only unused remaining hours, documented in writing.
  • Completed deliverables already accepted by the client are not refundable.
  • Chargebacks should be discussed with us first at the contact details below so we can resolve the issue directly.
